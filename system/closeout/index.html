How a member closes the week

Every Saturday each member closes out their week in private, then shares a short summary with their team. Free and paid members bring in their bank activity differently. After that, everyone follows the same steps.

Amber boxes are private; blue boxes are public. A dashed border marks a step a free member does by hand every week.

Free or paid: what's different

Free memberPaid member
Bank and card activityDownload a file from your bank's website each week and upload it to your private GitHub folderLink your accounts once; your AI reads them each week
Extra time each weekA few minutesNone after setup
Pending chargesOnly if your bank's file includes themRead and kept separate
Everything after thatThe same close-out, the same packet, the same team report

Paid Claude plans can link bank and card accounts through Plaid with Claude's Personal Finance feature. It is read-only, and Claude never sees your bank password. See how to set it up. Other AI services may offer their own bank link. Free members lose nothing but a few minutes, and the team report never shows who is free or paid.

What stays private, what is shared

  • Private, always: your bank activity, receipts, dollar amounts, names, addresses and account numbers. They stay in your private GitHub folder and your AI project.
  • The handoff packet: a short summary of shares and findings (for example, how each $100 of your taxes splits between governments). You read it and carry it to your team project yourself. Nothing is sent automatically.
  • Public: the team report and your team member page, built only from packets members chose to share.

Words used on this page

  • GitHub folder (techies call it a "repo," short for repository): an online folder that keeps every version of its files. A private one only you can open. A public one anyone can read.
  • AI project: a workspace in your AI app (Claude, ChatGPT or another) where it keeps your files and instructions.
  • Plaid: the service banks use to let an app read your accounts. Read-only: it cannot move money.
  • Close-out: the Saturday check that finishes your week's records and locks them.
  • Handoff packet: the short summary you read and carry from your private work to your team.
