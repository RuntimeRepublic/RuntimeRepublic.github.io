How the whole system works

Top to bottom: what governs everything and where each piece lives, how your money is traced, how progress is shared, how teams grow, and how citizens hold government accountable. Every part lives in public on GitHub, except your personal finances, which stay private.

1. The layers

Scripture is the chief control, and every rule must pass the same six values, the Regulative Principles of Liberty:

  1. God makes the rules. Rulers are ministers, not owners.
  2. Authorized means permitted. What is not written down is forbidden to government.
  3. No elite capture. No one adds rules for others beyond what is written.
  4. Rain on the just and the unjust. The same rule for everyone.
  5. Enumerate, limit, protect. Every power is listed and limited; life, liberty and property are protected.
  6. Remember. History is read through Scripture and taught to the next generation (Psalm 78).

Three frameworks apply those values: NBLF (AGCL) for government, NBBF for budgets and NBEF for education. Runtime Republic provides free tools, public data and publishing; its rules are in Runtime-Republic-Controls. Teams and members do the work and speak for themselves. Every member starts by building a master plan and adopting the six values; founders hold the same values.

3. How every dollar is traced

Your AI reads each receipt, estimates every tax (sales tax, fuel tax, fees), and points each line to a public rate with its source. Your dashboard then ranks the four budgets: Federal, State, Local and Family. The goal is to put Family first. Amber boxes are private; blue boxes are public.

4. How progress is shared each week

Nothing leaves your hands until you read it and push it yourself. The public sees group counts and weekly wins, never one person's numbers. See every week.

5. How teams grow

Teams: current status and the full team workflow

Every team has a sponsor, two admins, its own join page and social media page, and a line back to Team America. Teams split after seven members. Organizations whose purpose supports an AGCL point can join as qualified organizations and keep their own repositories.

6. How citizens hold government accountable

The ladder follows the founders' own sequence in the Declaration of the Causes and Necessity of Taking Up Arms (1775) and the Declaration of Independence (1776). Teams work like the 1772 committees of correspondence, and the Revere Network passes along a failed test only after a second team confirms it. Private persons never take the sword.
