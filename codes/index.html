Cheat codes

Type the code at the start of a message in your AI project. Works on a phone or a computer, and works best on a phone: open your project, start a new chat, add any photos, then type the code.

Every day

  • Check: Two-minute balance check and bills due soon.
  • Dashboard: Show your dashboard on screen. Read only.

Documents

  • Paystub: Read attached pay stubs: pay, every deduction, who gets paid first.
  • Bill: Read a bill: taxes, government fees and provider charges listed separately.
  • Receipt: Read a receipt photo and say which week it belongs to.
  • pfmlr Personal finance management, log receipt. Take one or more photos, then type the code: each photo is a separate receipt. Your AI shows the details for you to confirm.
  • pfmlr1 Same, but all the photos are one receipt (for a long receipt in several shots).

Saving to your records

  • Save it Save the result for your review. You get links; nothing is final until you merge.
  • + Yes, push it: after you check the details, save them to your GitHub repo.
  • Tips off Stop showing the GitHub how-to tips.

Share

  • @ After any save, write a very short post for Facebook or X about what you just did. It always ends with the link runtimerepublic.github.io.

Every week

  • Close week: Saturday night: finish the week's review and mark the week closed.
  • New week: Sunday: create the new week's folder.

Anytime

  • Status: Your levels and your next step on one screen.
  • Help: List every code.

Teams

  • ack: <name> Welcome a new member by name, so they know the AI → GitHub → AI loop works (Lean Civics onboarding check).

Recruiting (for organizers)

Attach a screenshot of the person's post or profile, then type the code. Every ask tells the person they also need a free GitHub account and an AI that connects to it.

  • ask: A short, friendly message asking whether they use Claude or another AI, to see if they're a fit for a team.
  • askfs: A strong ask to join the FairTax team, with one current event about the national debt.
  • askpks: An ask to join the Kansas-wide property tax group.
  • askpkssg: An ask to join the Sedgwick County chapter of the Kansas property tax group.

Rules that always apply: the AI never moves money or changes accounts, and nothing is saved or shared until you say so.
