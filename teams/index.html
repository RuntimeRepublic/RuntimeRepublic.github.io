How a team runs the challenge

  1. Every member starts with a master plan

    Build your own private master plan and adopt the six values (RP1 to RP6). Founders do the same. See Get ready now.

  2. Ask for a team

    Follow Bring your group: two doors, six gates. Say what the team is for. The purpose is checked against the control documents, the registry is checked for a team that already does the same thing, and a sponsor is named.

  3. Stand it up

    A public team repository holds MEMBERS.md, weekly counts (data/group-counts.csv) and reviewed study files. Every team keeps two admins so it never gets locked out. The team is added to the registry by pull request, and then it shows in the table above.

  4. Onboard each member

    The team lead opens an ONBOARD: <name> issue. The new member's AI reads the team README and the member posts onboarded. Every current member types ack: <name> to their AI and posts the line, with no deadline. The team: code shows who has not posted yet. That proves everyone's AI → GitHub → AI loop works.

  5. Run the week

    WhenWhoWhat
    During the weekEach memberSave receipts and bills to your private repo (pfmlr, Bill:, Paystub:).
    SaturdayEach memberClose week: review and close your week.
    SaturdayEach memberIf you choose, push broad category counts to the team by hand. Never names, accounts or single bills.
    SundayTeamUpdate the team counts and post the week's wins. New week: starts the next week.

  6. Climb the levels

    Members move up the personal finance levels and the Close Encounters levels (CE1 to CE5). The team publishes only how many members are at each level.

  7. Review and improve

    Once a month the team asks: what worked, what didn't, what we change next (Plan, Do, Check, Act). Changes are made by pull request, so every change is recorded.

The ground rules

  • Same rules for every member, founders included.
  • Decisions are made in public issues, not private messages.
  • Anyone may leave and take their own work with them.
  • No one's personal finances are ever shared. Only counts and wins.
