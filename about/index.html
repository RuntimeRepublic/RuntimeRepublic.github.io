Runtime Republic

Free tools that help people, teams and organizations organize online. You bring a free GitHub account and the AI assistant you already use. We bring templates, checklists and shared public data, so you spend less time on busywork and more time on what matters to you.

What we do

For people

Track every dollar with AI, and find out who gets paid first in your house: the government or your family. Your records stay private.

Join the Civic Encounters Challenge (launching December 1, 2026)

For teams

Start a Study Circle with friends and neighbors. Each team speaks for itself, keeps its own page, and tracks its progress in the open.

For Bible study groups

Organize your group online: meeting times, reading plans, shared notes and who's bringing what. Prayer requests and personal matters stay private to your group.

For organizations

Organizations whose purpose supports a point in the AGCL control documents can become Qualified Organizations. You keep your own repositories, brand, members and money. We provide free templates, shared government data and office hours.

For app developers

Our government data is public and free to use. Find the work that eats people's time and build something that saves it. Every developer is listed under the same rules.

What we believe

  • You speak for yourself. Only a team's members speak for their team. No team speaks for another.
  • Evidence first. Every number has a source and a date. Blank is better than a guess.
  • Your information is yours. Originals stay with you. Only summaries you approve leave your hands.
  • Every rule is public. Our rules and every change to them are on GitHub.
