Preamble

When in the course of human events it becomes necessary for free people to watch over the money they are taxed and to hold their governments to account, a decent respect for one another requires that they declare the duties they accept. We hold these truths to be self-evident: that each person is the steward of their own life, liberty and property, and that each Team governs itself by the consent of its members.

Runtime Republic is dedicated to uniting peaceful associations of citizens into one class of taxpayer; to event-based spending, with exit points in every relationship between citizens and every level of government; and to total taxpayer supremacy. Governments are stewards, not owners, of our tax dollars. Governments are established by a binding covenant between the people and the current officeholders, cannot be altered without a constitutional change in the organic law, and at every level must act and respond as republics. Each government gets one event to spend our tax dollars, and only on enumerated items. We, the taxpayers, are the owners.

Our property, including the taxes we have paid in the past, is ours. No government account that is not backed by the people's property can be considered lawful. The first duty of every government is to restore all property rights, including those pooled in tax bait-and-switch schemes, above all Social Security and Medicare. We refuse to walk away.

It is likewise the solemn duty of government to establish a currency indexed to gold, and changed only by an increase in gold, to secure honest money owed to the people and demanded by God Himself: "A false balance is abomination to the LORD: but a just weight is his delight" (Proverbs 11:1, KJV; see also Leviticus 19:35–36 and Deuteronomy 25:13–15). No government shall ever tax future generations, and every level of government must move toward a future powered by savings, not taxes.

Every taxpayer holds clawback rights: any tax dollar assigned outside enumerated authority is removed from government control and follows the taxpayer's standing instruction, to retire the national debt or to provide goods through a qualified charity or church (AGCL Point 05.2). Every taxpayer holds sinking-fund rights: an individually owned, inheritable Sinking Liberty Fund, credited from the taxes we pay, protected from seizure, from which government must borrow first and repay on schedule (AGCL Point 05.1). Every level of government must function as a Runtime Republic: every power enumerated and limited, every dollar traceable on a public ledger, every relationship with an exit point, and every rule backed by a consequence. Rules without consequences are suggestions.

We, the Members of Runtime Republic, in Order to form a more perfect Union, do ordain and establish this Agreement.

The preamble states purpose and creates no rights or duties. Read each pair of boxes left to right, top to bottom. If the two columns differ, the left column controls. Bracketed items [like this] are for the attorney to confirm.
