Register a team

  1. Build your own master plan first and adopt the six values. See Get ready now.
  2. Say what the team is for. The purpose is checked against the control documents, and this registry is checked for a team that already does the same thing.
  3. Name a sponsor (a registered team or the founder) and two admins, so the team never gets locked out.
  4. Stand up a public team repository with MEMBERS.md, data/group-counts.csv and a packets/ folder. Copy Team America as the example.
  5. Open a pull request that adds one line to Registry/teams.csv. Once it is merged, the team shows on this page, the Teams page and the dashboard.

Status words (proposed): Forming means fewer than two admins or no closed week yet. Active means the team posts weekly counts. Paused and Closed mean what they say. Rules: Runtime-Republic-Controls, CONTROL 07.
