Bring your group

Starting a team, or bringing a group you already have? Everyone comes in through one of two doors, then passes the same six gates in order. The Team Registry shows which gate each team is on.

Proposed. The rules are in Runtime-Republic-Controls, CONTROL 07, section 3.9.

Pick your door

ANew team

A few people starting a team from scratch.

Start: one of you opens the team request (Gate 0).

BExisting group

A Facebook group, a coalition, a club, or an issue team such as FairTax.

Start: your group's contact opens the team request and names your group (Gate 0).

If you are an existing group (Door B)

  • Keep what you have. Your group keeps its name, its platform and its leaders. Your Runtime Republic team is a separate team with its own public repository.
  • Nobody joins as a bloc. Each person adopts the values and joins on their own. A leader cannot sign up the members.
  • No chain of command carries over. The team's two admins are chosen from members who passed Gate 1.
  • Neither speaks for the other. The team speaks for itself, and your group speaks for itself.
  • Settle conflicts first. If your group's rules conflict with the six values, say so in the request. It is settled before Gate 2.

Why: liberty is the art of elite capture avoidance. These rules keep any group from capturing its members or Runtime Republic, and Runtime Republic from capturing your group.

The six gates

  1. Gate 0 · Request

    Open an issue titled TEAM REQUEST: <name> in Runtime-Republic-Controls. Say what the team is for, your door (A or B), your level (nation, state, county or city) and who you'd like as sponsor.

    Checked by: the founder or a sponsor

  2. Gate 1 · Values

    At least two founding members each build a private master plan and sign the six values (RP1 to RP6) in their own repository. See Get ready now.

    Checked by: the sponsor

  3. Gate 2 · Purpose and sponsor

    Your purpose is checked against the three frameworks' control documents, and the registry is checked for a team already doing the same thing. Your sponsor accepts. You pick your team name and your two admins.

    Checked by: the sponsor

  4. Gate 3 · Stand up

    Make a public team repository like Team America, with MEMBERS.md, data/group-counts.csv and a packets/ folder. Both admins get access. Add your team to the registry by pull request.

    Checked by: the founder

  5. Gate 4 · Loop proven

    Every founding member passes the onboarding test (ONBOARD: issue, then ack:) and sets up two AI projects: Personal Finance and Team.

    Checked by: the team lead

  6. Gate 5 · First closed week

    At least two members file their weekly packets for the same week, and the team posts its first counts. Your team appears on the dashboard and changes from Forming to Active.

    Checked by: the team lead, reported to the sponsor

Status words

Forming: working through the gates. Active: passed Gate 5 and posted counts at the latest weekly close. Paused: missed the latest weekly close, or has fewer than two admins. A paused team is Active again the next week it posts with two admins. Closed: the team ended; its repository stays public as a record.
