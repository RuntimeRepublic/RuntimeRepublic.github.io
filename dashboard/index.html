How to read the levels and ratings

Every member's progress has two numbers: a level and an impact rating.

  • The level (CE1 to CE5) says what kind of encounter you have had. CE1 is noticing that government takes your money. CE2 is a real budget with receipts. CE3 is naming every government that takes from you. CE4 is talking with the officials who set your taxes. CE5 is changing the law itself. Levels are earned in order.
  • The impact rating (1 to 5) says how far that encounter reached. A 1 is the first step at that level. A 5 is the most that level can do. In CE5, a 1 is citizens and elected officials writing a bill together openly on GitHub; a 5 is a whole system where the Constitution, House and Senate Rules, and laws all line up with the three frameworks.
  • Reading a box: ✓3 means the level is earned at impact rating 3. … means working on it. An empty box means not yet.
  • Reading the team row: the big number is how many members have earned that level; "impact" is their average rating.
  • Earned, not claimed. Every level and every rating needs evidence, and a reviewer checks it before it shows here.
  • Post it as level and rating, for example "CE3, rating 2."
